How your progress works

Every mission moves your pilot forward.

⚡

Mission progress

Correct answers build progress, and a perfect mission adds a bonus. Higher mission levels increase your rewards.

✦

Adaptive challenges

Each subject remembers its latest star rating separately. Earn 4–5 stars to make the next challenge harder, 0–2 stars to make it easier, or 3 stars to repeat the same difficulty with new questions.

◈

Coins

Earn coins based on your accuracy and challenge difficulty, with bonuses for fast perfect missions and achievements. Spend coins on themes, avatar frames, and mouse trails.

🔥

Streaks

Complete a mission each day to keep your streak. A missed UTC day resets it; a three-day streak earns an achievement.

No timer, no pressure — just you getting stronger with every try.
