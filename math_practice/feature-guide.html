Math Galaxy feature guide

A guide to the practice games, pilot progression, titles, rewards, and account features currently in Math Galaxy.

Math missions

Addition · 5 levels

Level 1: one-digit + one-digit; Level 2: one-digit + two-digit; Level 3: two-digit + two-digit; Level 4: three-digit + two-digit; Level 5: three-digit + three-digit.

Subtraction · 5 levels

Level 1: one-digit − one-digit; Level 2: two-digit − one-digit; Level 3: two-digit − two-digit; Level 4: three-digit − two-digit; Level 5: three-digit − three-digit. Problems avoid negative answers.

Multiplication · 2 levels

Level 1: one-digit × one-digit; Level 2: two-digit × one-digit.

Division · 2 levels

Level 1: two-digit ÷ one-digit; Level 2: two-digit ÷ two-digit. Questions use whole-number quotients.

Each mission has 10 questions. You can start up to two tries per subject and level each UTC day. Starting a mission uses a try, even if you leave before finishing; the limit is shared across devices and resets at midnight UTC. The game shows your remaining tries, and the home page disables Play when both tries for the next challenge have been used.

Choose a subject and press Play; the game selects the difficulty automatically. Each subject tracks its own most recent star rating: 4–5 stars raises the next challenge by one level, 0–2 stars lowers it by one, and 3 stars keeps the level while giving you a different set of questions. Difficulty stays within that subject's available levels.

Enter a non-negative whole-number answer, submit it to move to the next question, or use the hint and new-mission controls. At the end, see your answers, correctness, answer times, accuracy, speed, and a 0–5 star rating.

Pilot levels and titles

Correct answers earn progress points, with an extra bonus for getting all 10 correct. Rewards scale with the mission level. The level requirement rises by 150 points each time: Level 2 takes 150 total points, Level 3 takes 450 total, Level 4 takes 900 total, and Level 5 takes 1,500 total. In general, moving from Level N to N + 1 costs 150 × N additional points.

The profile progress bar shows how far you are through the current pilot level and how many points remain. Pilot levels continue to determine title milestones; challenge difficulty adapts independently for each subject.

Milestone titles

  • Level 1: Cadet in training
  • Level 10: New Pilot
  • Level 20: Amateur Pilot
  • Level 30: Good Pilot
  • Level 40: Great Pilot
  • Level 50: Pro Pilot
  • Level 60: Elite Pilot
  • Level 70: Legend
  • Level 80: Mythical
  • Level 90: Cosmic Pilot
  • Level 100: Galactic Legend

Every 10 levels after 100 earns the next numbered Galactic Legend title. A newly unlocked title appears in an animated notification on the right, with white sparks.

Lucky title

Lucky is a separate, permanent title. While signed in and active in a visible Math Galaxy page, the game checks once per minute for a 1% chance to earn it—including during a mission. Activity means keyboard, pointer, touch, or scrolling input; checks stop after three minutes without activity. Winning displays the same right-side title notification and sparks.

The Lucky check is performed by the Math Galaxy service and is limited to one roll per minute per pilot.

Coins, achievements, and daily streaks

Coins

A mission awards 5 coins plus 2 per correct answer, multiplied by its level. Achievement coins and a random coin drop are added; the random drop also scales with mission level.

Achievements

  • First Mission — complete one mission; 10 coins.
  • Perfect Mission — get all 10 right; 20 coins.
  • Three-Day Streak — practice three consecutive days; 30 coins.
  • Mission Regular — complete 10 missions; 40 coins.
  • Century Club — answer 100 questions correctly; 50 coins.

Daily streak

Completing at least one mission keeps the streak going for that UTC day. Missing a UTC day resets it. Your current and longest streaks are shown in your profile.

Random coin drops

Each completed mission has a 70% chance of no extra coins, 25% chance of 5 extra, 4% chance of 15 extra, or 1% chance of 50 extra, before the mission-level multiplier.

Pilot account and cosmetics

  • Create an account or sign in to save progress across sessions.
  • The profile shows your title, level progress, level, coins, and day streak.
  • Profile tabs include Progress, Awards, Leaderboard, Friends, and Shop.
  • The global leaderboard lists up to 25 pilots. The Friends leaderboard compares you with your friends (up to 50 friends).
  • Add friends by pilot username.
  • Spend coins on profile themes, avatar frames, and mouse trails. Purchased cosmetics can be equipped from the Shop tab.

Shop items

  • Comet Trail theme — 50 coins.
  • Lunar Frame — 100 coins.
  • Diamond Trail — 150 coins.
  • Nebula Theme — 200 coins.

Rating details

Accuracy contributes up to 80 rating points and average answer speed contributes up to 20. The final score is converted into zero to five stars.
