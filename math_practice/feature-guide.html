Math Galaxy feature guide

A private, organized reference to Math Galaxy’s practice games, adaptive difficulty, limits, progression, rewards, and account features.

1. Subjects and missions

Addition · 5 difficulties

Level 1: one-digit + one-digit; Level 2: one-digit + two-digit; Level 3: two-digit + two-digit; Level 4: three-digit + two-digit; Level 5: three-digit + three-digit.

Subtraction · 5 difficulties

Level 1: one-digit − one-digit; Level 2: two-digit − one-digit; Level 3: two-digit − two-digit; Level 4: three-digit − two-digit; Level 5: three-digit − three-digit. Problems avoid negative answers.

Multiplication · 2 difficulties

Level 1: one-digit × one-digit; Level 2: two-digit × one-digit.

Division · 2 difficulties

Level 1: two-digit ÷ one-digit; Level 2: two-digit ÷ two-digit. Questions use whole-number quotients.

Press Play on a subject card to start. Difficulty is chosen automatically; there are no level buttons to select.

2. Adaptive challenge difficulty

Each subject saves its own most recently completed challenge level and star rating. An addition result does not change the next subtraction challenge.

  • 4 or 5 stars: the next challenge in that subject moves up one difficulty.
  • 0, 1, or 2 stars: the next challenge moves down one difficulty.
  • 3 stars: stay at the same difficulty and get a different set of questions.

Difficulty stays within that subject's available range. The subject card shows your last challenge's stars and level. The server stores this history with your account.

A new subject starts at its first difficulty. Difficulty never goes below Level 1 or above the subject's maximum. Pilot level and subject difficulty are separate.

3. Daily challenge tries

  • You may start up to two tries per subject and difficulty per UTC day.
  • A try is used when a mission starts, even if it is abandoned before completion.
  • The limit is enforced by the server, shared across devices, and resets at midnight UTC.
  • The mission shows remaining tries. The home page checks the next adaptive challenge and disables Play if both tries for it have been used.

If a completed mission changes the next difficulty, the try counter follows that new subject-and-difficulty combination.

4. Playing and mission results

Every mission contains 10 questions. Enter a non-negative whole-number answer and submit it to continue. Hints are available, and a new-mission button starts another challenge at the automatically selected difficulty.

At the end, review each question, your submitted and correct answers, and how long each answer took. The result also shows accuracy, speed, a 0–100 rating, and zero to five stars.

How stars are calculated

Accuracy contributes up to 80 rating points; average answer time contributes up to 20. The total maps to stars: 0–29 points = 0 stars, 30–44 = 1, 45–59 = 2, 60–74 = 3, 75–89 = 4, and 90–100 = 5.

5. Pilot progress and levels

Correct answers earn progress points, with an extra bonus for answering all 10 correctly. Both rewards scale with challenge difficulty. Pilot levels track overall progress and unlock title milestones; they do not set subject difficulty.

The next pilot level requires 150 more progress points per level number: Level 2 takes 150 total points, Level 3 takes 450 total, Level 4 takes 900 total, and Level 5 takes 1,500 total. In general, going from pilot Level N to N + 1 costs 150 × N additional points.

Your profile progress bar shows progress through the current pilot level and how many points remain.

6. Pilot titles

Level milestone titles

  • Level 1: Cadet in training
  • Level 10: New Pilot
  • Level 20: Amateur Pilot
  • Level 30: Good Pilot
  • Level 40: Great Pilot
  • Level 50: Pro Pilot
  • Level 60: Elite Pilot
  • Level 70: Legend
  • Level 80: Mythical
  • Level 90: Cosmic Pilot
  • Level 100: Galactic Legend

Every 10 levels after 100 earns the next numbered Galactic Legend title. A newly unlocked title appears in an animated notification on the right, with white sparks.

Lucky — a separate title

While signed in and active on a visible Math Galaxy page, the game checks once per minute for a 1% chance to earn Lucky, including during a mission. Keyboard, pointer, touch, or scrolling input counts as activity. Checks stop after three minutes without activity. Lucky is permanent and appears in the same animated notification with white sparks.

The Lucky check is performed by the Math Galaxy service and is limited to one roll per minute per pilot.

7. Coins and random drops

A mission awards 5 coins plus 2 per correct answer, multiplied by challenge difficulty. Achievement rewards and a random drop are added; random drops also scale with mission difficulty.

  • 70% chance of no extra coins.
  • 25% chance of 5 extra coins.
  • 4% chance of 15 extra coins.
  • 1% chance of 50 extra coins.

8. Achievements

  • First Mission: complete your first mission; 10 coins.
  • Perfect Mission: answer all 10 correctly; 20 coins.
  • Three-Day Streak: practice on three consecutive days; 30 coins.
  • Mission Regular: complete 10 missions; 40 coins.
  • Century Club: answer 100 questions correctly; 50 coins.

Unlocked awards and their coin rewards appear in the profile's Awards tab.

9. Daily practice streak

Completing at least one mission each UTC day keeps your streak. Missing a UTC day resets it. Your current and longest streaks appear in your profile. The Three-Day Streak achievement rewards three consecutive practice days.

10. Pilot account and social features

  • Create an account or sign in to save progress across sessions.
  • The profile shows your title, level progress, level, coins, and day streak.
  • Profile tabs include Progress, Awards, Leaderboard, Friends, and Shop.
  • The global leaderboard lists up to 25 pilots. The Friends leaderboard compares you with your friends (up to 50 friends).
  • Add friends by pilot username.

11. Cosmetic shop

Spend coins on cosmetics. Once purchased, items can be equipped from the Shop tab.

Available items

  • Comet Trail theme — 50 coins.
  • Lunar Frame — 100 coins.
  • Diamond Trail — 150 coins.
  • Nebula Theme — 200 coins.

Cosmetic types

The Comet Trail and Nebula Theme change the profile theme. Lunar Frame decorates the avatar. Diamond Trail adds a cursor trail.
